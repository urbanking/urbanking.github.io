I work on autonomous driving in mixed traffic, where automated vehicles share the road with human drivers. I'm interested in predicting how surrounding road users will move, planning around them, and the simulations used to train and test both, including how well those results carry over to real roads.

I started my M.S. at KAIST in March 2026 and take part in three national R&D projects on autonomous driving. Before that I studied Urban Planning and Engineering at Yonsei University, with a minor in Applied Statistics. My capstone there used LLM-based persona simulation to forecast demand for autonomous ride-hailing.

Research interests

  • Behavior prediction
  • Motion planning in mixed traffic
  • AI-based transportation simulation
  • Sim-to-real
  • Closed-loop simulation
