Research

I'm in the first year of my M.S. and working toward research on autonomous driving in mixed traffic. Right now I'm reading the literature, learning traffic simulators such as SUMO and VISSIM, and contributing to the lab's national R&D projects below.

Interests

  • Behavior prediction

    Forecasting what surrounding vehicles and pedestrians will do next.

  • Motion planning in mixed traffic

    Deciding how an automated vehicle should act when it shares intersections with human drivers.

  • AI-based transportation simulation

    Traffic simulation with learned behavior models and agents, used to train and evaluate driving policies.

  • Sim-to-real and closed-loop evaluation

    Whether behavior learned in simulation holds on real roads, and testing prediction and planning while the rest of traffic reacts, not only on logged data.
